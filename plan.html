The plan

See where you are going, before you get there.

Your forecast starts from books that are already closed, so it is built on real numbers. Drag across the chart to move from the past into the plan.

Your daily view

What is behind you, what needs you, and what is ahead.

One screen to start the day. The month that just closed, the few things waiting for you, and what is coming over the horizon.

Analytics

The dashboard for your kind of business.

The measures that matter are different for software, a store, a services firm or a cafe. Each comes ready with its own eight, built on the same books. Any other business starts from a general set and swaps in its own.

Scenarios

Three futures, one set of books.

Build each case from real decisions: hire, raise, change prices, lose a customer. Compare them on cash, revenue, burn and people, see what choosing each would cost, and find the assumption each one is most exposed to.

Revenue forecast

One forecast, built two ways.

Build it up from the customers you have, the deals already open and what the team can sell. Or start from a goal and see what it takes. Good plans check one against the other.

Headcount planning

Plan the team, see the runway.

Hiring is more than engineers. Add people by role and see what each does to payroll, revenue and how long the cash lasts.

Financial model

The spreadsheet investors ask for, already built.

A full three-statement operating model for 24 months: revenue build, people, income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, in three scenarios, with live formulas and an Excel export.

Board packs

The board pack builds itself.

A real deck, not a data dump, with a template for each kind of business. Pick the slides, and each one fills itself from the ledger and the plan, with the story drafted for you to edit.

Data room

A data room that is always ready.

Accounts, model, contracts, cap table and company records in one place, kept current from the system. Share it by link, see who read what, and answer their questions from the ledger.

Ask

Ask the ledger, out loud if you like.

Tap the microphone and ask out loud, or type. Ask what happened and the books answer. Ask what happens next and the plan answers. Either way you get a sentence, the numbers and the working.

Know where you have been. See where you are going.
