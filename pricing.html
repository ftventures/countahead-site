Questions

Asked before you have to.

How do you measure my company's size?

By the higher of your revenue or your spending over the last twelve months. Both figures are already in your books, so there is nothing to fill in or declare.

Why not revenue alone?

Because a funded startup with forty people and no revenue is not a small customer, and a shop with thin margins is not a big one. Taking the higher of the two is fair to both.

What happens when we grow?

Nothing, until your renewal. Your size is checked once a year. If you have moved into the next size, the price changes then, and you are told thirty days ahead.

What if we shrink?

Ask for an early recheck, once a year. If you have moved into a smaller size, the lower price applies from your next month on monthly, and from your renewal on yearly.

We are above $50 million. What do we pay?

We agree it with you. Everything is still included, and you get a named contact.

Is anything held back for bigger companies?

No. The books, the plan, revenue, contracts, the cap table and the data room are in every price. Three things change with size: how many companies in a group are included, the allowance for heavier AI work, and the kind of support.

Do you charge per user?

No. Invite everyone who needs to see the numbers.

What is the allowance for heavier AI work?

Everyday work by the agents is included. A few bigger jobs that you ask for, such as a written review of your plan, come with a monthly allowance that is larger for larger companies. We will publish the amounts before launch.

We have more than one company. How does that work?

Your size is measured across the whole group, and each company keeps its own books. Two companies are included at the two smallest sizes, then three, five and ten as the size goes up. Each extra company adds 20% of your monthly price. A company with no activity in the year is free.

Do I still need my accountant?

Yes. Agents take the entry work. Review, judgment, tax filings and advice stay with a qualified person, and their seat here is free. The price on this page is for the software.

Do I have to drop my CRM or my signing tool?

No. Deals and signing are built in, and many small companies will find them enough. If your sales team relies on a bigger CRM, keep it and connect it.

What does switching cost?

Nothing. Your history comes across and each month is checked against your old books. You pay only after a month has closed here and agreed to the cent.

Can I buy it today?

Not yet. Countahead is still being built. These are the prices we plan to launch with, and people on the early access list will hear first if anything changes.

Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yes. Pay for the year and you pay for ten months, so two are free. It is the only discount we offer. There are no introductory prices that jump later.

What if we pay yearly and then leave?

Yearly is a commitment, and the two free months are what you get for making it. Unused months are not refunded. You keep full access until the end of the year you paid for, and your books export in full at any time. If you are not sure yet, start on monthly and switch to yearly later. Billing and refunds.
