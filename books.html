Approvals

Approve two hundred entries in one click.

Nothing posts without an approval, but that should not mean two hundred clicks. Routine work is grouped for one decision, and the few that deserve a look are pulled out.

Controls and audit trail

Agents propose. People approve.

Automation is only useful in finance if you can see exactly what it did and why. So control is how the product is built, not a setting you switch on.

Countahead reduces the manual work. It does not replace your accountant, tax adviser or statutory filings, and it makes working with them easier.

Policies

Write the rule once. The software follows it.

Each policy sets how the system behaves and is published in plain words for the whole company, so everyone knows how spending, discounts and collections work.

Audit readiness

Run your own audit before the auditors do.

An agent runs the checks an auditor would and prepares the evidence for each, so the real audit starts with the answers already on the table.
