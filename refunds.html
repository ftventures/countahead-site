Billing

Billing and refunds

Last updated November 22, 2026. Short version: nothing is on sale yet. When it is, monthly can be cancelled any time, and yearly is a one-year commitment.

Where things stand

Countahead is not on sale yet. Nobody has been charged, so there is nothing to refund today. This page sets out the billing and refund terms we plan to launch with, so you can read them before you decide anything. The final version will be confirmed when paid plans open.

When billing starts

You pay nothing while your history is brought across and checked. Billing starts only after a month has closed in Countahead and agreed with your previous books. If that never happens, you are never charged.

If you pay monthly

There is no contract. You can cancel at any time, and the cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have already paid for. We do not refund part of a month.

If you pay yearly

Yearly is a one-year commitment, and the two free months are what you get for making it. Unused months are not refunded if you leave early. You keep full access until the end of the year you paid for.

If you are not sure yet, start on monthly. You can switch to yearly later.

When we do refund

  • If we charge you by mistake, or twice, we refund it in full.
  • If we ever close the service, we refund the unused part of anything you have paid in advance.

If your company grows or shrinks

Your size is checked once a year, at renewal, and you are told the result thirty days ahead. If you have moved into the next size, the new price applies from that renewal. We never charge the difference for the months before it.

If your company has shrunk into a smaller size, you can ask for one early recheck a year. On monthly the lower price applies from your next month. On yearly it applies from your renewal.

If a payment fails

We try again over the following two weeks and tell you each time. If it is still unpaid after 21 days, the account becomes read only: you can see and export everything, but nothing new is posted. Full access returns as soon as the payment goes through.

More than one company

A group is sized on its total. Two companies are included at the two smallest sizes, then three, five and ten. Each extra company adds 20% of the monthly price. A company with no activity in the year is not charged.

Your books are yours

Cancelling does not hold your data hostage. You can export your books in full at any time while your plan is active. How long they stay available after a plan ends will be stated here before launch.

How to ask

Write to hello@countahead.ai from the email on your account and tell us what happened. A person will answer.

Your legal rights

Nothing on this page takes away rights you have under the law of the country where you live.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with it. A change never applies to a period you have already paid for.
