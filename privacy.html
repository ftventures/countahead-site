Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated November 22, 2026. Short version: we keep the email you give us for early access, and nothing else.

Who is responsible for your data

Countahead is a trading name of Oasis Ventures FZE (license no. 10594, Umm Al Quwain Free Trade Zone Authority), Al Shmookh Business Center, One UAQ, UAQ Free Trade Zone, Umm Al Quwain, United Arab Emirates. Oasis Ventures FZE decides how the personal data described here is used. For any privacy question or request, write to hello@countahead.ai.

What we collect

If you ask for early access, we collect your work email, the role you pick if you pick one, the page you signed up from and the time. We use this to send you one confirmation email, to write to you when early access opens and to understand who is interested. We do not sell it and we do not share it for advertising.

We collect nothing else. There are no accounts on this site yet.

What stays on your device

The demos on this site run in your browser on sample data. What you type into them, including questions in the ask box, is not sent to us.

If you use the microphone in a demo, your browser's built-in speech recognition turns your speech into text. Depending on your browser, that may be processed by your browser's provider, for example Google or Apple. Countahead does not record, store or receive your audio or the text.

Who processes your data

  • Early access sign-ups are stored in a database run by Supabase, hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • The confirmation email is sent through Resend, which processes your email address in the United States to deliver it. We do not track whether you open it.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google and shares your IP address with it.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and there is no analytics script on this site.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up until you ask us to remove it, or until we stop offering early access.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time and wherever you live. Write to hello@countahead.ai and we will do it.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
