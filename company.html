Company

Ready for investors, every day.

Your cap table, option grants, data room, board minutes and filing deadlines, kept current from the same books and the same plan. A fundraise, a board meeting or an audit starts from what is already there.

Who owns the companySample data
  • Founders60%
  • Seed investors25%
  • Options granted9%
  • Options available6%

A record, not the legal register. Every entry links to its approval and its signed document.

Cap table

Who owns what, and what a round would change.

Shares, options and investors in one table that agrees to the books. Move the sliders to model a raise.

Options

Every grant, and what has vested.

The option register with vesting worked out for each person, the monthly expense posted to the books, and new grants drafted with the board consent they need.

Data room

A data room that is always ready.

Accounts, model, contracts, cap table and company records in one place, kept current from the system. Share it by link, see who read what, and answer their questions from the ledger.

Company records

The documents, read and remembered.

Shareholder agreements, the option plan, board minutes and filings, with the key terms pulled out and the deadlines tracked.

Drafting

From rough notes to a formal document.

Drop in a call transcript, an email thread or your own notes. An agent drafts the minutes, the resolution or the action list, checks it against your records and leaves the sign-off to you and your lawyer.

Reminders

Nothing about the company gets forgotten.

Board meetings, filings, vesting dates, renewals and investor updates, read from your documents and put on one calendar.
