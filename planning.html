Why did gross margin decline last month?

Gross margin fell from 78.4% to 74.9% in November.

The decline is cost-driven. Hosting rose $11.2k after the usage tier change on November 3, and support contractor spend rose $3.3k. Revenue grew 4.0% over the same month, so pricing and volume are not the cause.
