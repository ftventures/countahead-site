Revenue

From signed deal to cash, without retyping it once.

The sale is where the numbers start. Capture it once, and the invoices, the cash, the revenue in the books and the forecast all follow from it.

Typed once, on the deal. Every later line is derived from it.

Pick how you sell

One record in. Everything else follows.

Subscriptions, usage, an online store, a till, services or plain invoices. Choose one and try it.

Customers

Everything starts with who you sell to.

Each customer with its people, contract, invoices, payments and history in one place, and a suggested next step. A basic CRM of its own, or kept in step with the one you already use.

Deals

A pipeline the forecast can read.

A simple pipeline of its own, or synced from HubSpot or Salesforce. Either way, the same deals feed the forecast and turn into contracts, and each one carries a health score that shows its reason.

Quotes

From quote to invoice, without retyping a field.

Build a quote from the price book and send it as a page the customer can accept. Acceptance wins the deal, and the invoice, the revenue schedule and the forecast follow from it. Step through one deal.

Contracts

Approve, sign, amend, renew.

Terms are filled in from the deal in your CRM and the customer signs on the platform. The signed contract is then the record, and every change to it flows through to billing, revenue and the forecast. Walk one through.

Collections

An agent that chases what you are owed.

It watches every unpaid invoice and drafts the follow-up, in the right tone, to the right person. You approve, or let the routine ones go on their own.

Revenue recognition

What you have been paid for and not yet earned.

Deferred revenue by customer and by type with the months it will be earned in, one price shared across several promises, and the sales number tied to the accounting number. It follows the five steps of ASC 606 and IFRS 15.

For subscription businesses

Recurring revenue, customer by customer.

Growth, retention and what moved this month, then the same revenue by customer, by cohort, by geography and by segment.

For sales teams

Commissions that calculate themselves.

Set the rules once: rates for new business and upsells, what happens above quota, when it is paid and when it is clawed back. Statements follow from signed contracts and cash received.
