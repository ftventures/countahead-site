People

Everyone who works here, and what they cost.

One record for the people in the company, tied to the plan, the books and the cap table. Your payroll provider keeps running payroll. Countahead keeps everything else about your team in step.

The team

One list of people. Every part of the product uses it.

The hiring plan, the payroll cost by department, who approves what, option grants and commissions all need to know who works here. Today each keeps its own list. Here there is one.

My page

Each person's own page, with nothing they should not see.

Expenses, approvals, equity, commission, documents and time off, gathered in one place. Pick a person and tap a tile.

Time off

A day off is also a number in the books.

Request, approve, see who is out. And because unused holiday is money the company owes, the month-end figure comes from the real balances. Send a request and watch it.

Joining and leaving

One change, everywhere it matters.

When someone joins or leaves, a dozen things have to happen across finance, access and equity. Tap through each list.

What stays outside

People, not an HR system.

Payroll and the big HR tools are good at what they do. We connect to them and stay out of their way.

Here
  • The person and position record, with cost by team.
  • Each person's own page.
  • Time off, with balances that feed the books.
  • Joining and leaving checklists.
  • People cost against plan, by position.
With your payroll or HR provider
  • Payroll, and anything that calculates or files payroll tax.
  • Benefits.
  • Performance reviews and goals.
  • Hiring and applicant tracking.
  • Advice on employment law. Leave allowances are the ones you set.

Planned connections: Gusto, Rippling, Deel and Remote. If you already track time off there, it stays there and the balances are read in. See integrations.
