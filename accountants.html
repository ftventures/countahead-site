What you are probably thinking

The hard questions, answered straight.

Is this built to replace me?

No. It is built to take the part of the job nobody trained for: collecting, coding, matching and chasing. Review, judgment, adjusting entries, sign-off, tax and advice stay with you. A company on Countahead needs its accountant as much as before, for better work.

Why would I trust an entry I did not make?

You should not, at first. A new client starts with everything waiting for approval. Automatic posting is unlocked one rule at a time, only after that rule has proved accurate on that client's own items, and it is withdrawn if corrections rise. Every entry opens to its document, and each bank account and sub-ledger must agree with the ledger before a month can close.

Can I see how accurate it is on my own client?

Yes. Accuracy is measured per rule and per client from what you approve, change and reject, and shown to you. We do not ask you to rely on an average from somebody else's books.

Who is responsible if an agent gets an entry wrong?

Responsibility stays where it is today. Management owns the books and you own your work. What changes is the evidence. Every entry shows who or what proposed it, the rule that allowed it and who approved it, so it is always clear what an agent prepared and what a person decided. A wrong entry is corrected by a reversal with a full trail, never by a silent edit.

Does the AI decide accounting treatment?

No. Classifying a funding instrument, setting a revenue policy, writing off a debt: the system prepares the facts and asks a person. Your decision is recorded with your name and reasoning, then applied consistently.

Does the AI do the arithmetic?

No. Balances, schedules, depreciation and currency calculations are ordinary code that gives the same answer every time, and each can be redone independently from its inputs. The AI reads documents, proposes and drafts.

What happens if the AI is unavailable?

Items queue for a person and everything else carries on. The books, the statements and the close do not depend on it.

Can I act as accountant and auditor for the same client?

No, and the system will not let a firm hold both roles for one client. Auditors get read-only access, full exports and samples, and can never post.

Will you go around me to my clients?

No. Countahead is software for a company and its accountant to use together. We do not rank or rate accountants, and we do not sell your clients a replacement for you.

Who owns the client relationship and the data?

The client owns its books. You are invited by them, exactly as today. You keep a record of your own work: your sign-offs, your review notes and the reports you issued.

What about my clients on QuickBooks or Xero?

Their history comes across read only, and every month is checked against the old books before anything is relied on. You can run both side by side until you are satisfied. See how switching works.

Do I have to give up my chart, my checklists, my way of working?

No. Bring your own chart of accounts, layouts, rules and close checklist as firm templates. Where a client's existing chart is a mess, a health check shows what to fix, and you decide what changes.

What about tax returns and filings?

They stay with you. Countahead keeps the books and the supporting schedules ready. It does not prepare or file returns and does not give tax advice.

What does it cost me?

Your own seat is free. Your client pays one price for their company, set by its size, with everything included. A volume price for firms that pay on behalf of clients is not set yet, and we would like your view on it. See pricing.

Is it live? Is it certified?

Neither yet. Countahead is in development and the screens on this site use sample data. No certification will be named until it exists. We would rather show you the design now and have you tell us where it is wrong.

Where we stand

What we will do, and what we will not.

We will
  • Send every judgment call to a person.
  • Show the evidence behind every entry.
  • Put your name on your reviews, adjustments and sign-offs.
  • Let you and your client take everything out, at any time.
  • Say plainly what is not built yet.
We will not
  • Claim to replace accountants or bookkeepers.
  • Give accounting, tax or legal advice.
  • Rank, rate or advertise accountants against each other.
  • Train AI models on your clients' data.
  • Name a certification before we hold it.
